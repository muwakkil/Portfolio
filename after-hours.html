About

This group case project was based on the issue of VCU Graphic Design Students’ creativity being stifled by the absence of a collaborative space. Our main goal was to create a space and influence people into using the space. We created a club based for graphic designers but open to all and held club meetings enticing people to come with different community activities. One of them, decorating and leaving a mark on a supply cart created to help students stay and do work in our building. Learned a lot about how to work and compromise within a group and to be efficient and effective with deliverables to motivate and influence others.
