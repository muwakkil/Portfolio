About

Stitched Stories is a website created to help educate people in an interactive setting. It uses traditional Palestinian embroidery. They signify resistance, identity, and storytelling, with motifs reflecting history, geography, and personal narratives. The goal is for the users to create a piece using the significant motifs that they can save into an archive and view other peoples creation.
