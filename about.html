About

As a freshly graduated graphic designer focused on visual systems, digital experiences, and UX/UI, I enjoy translating research and complex ideas into clear, engaging visual experiences. My work is informed by a range of visual influences, including Middle Eastern design, which I draw from as a source of inspiration in select cultural and visual projects. I approach design with curiosity and intention, exploring how visual language can communicate ideas, guide interaction, and create meaningful connections.
