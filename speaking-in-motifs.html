Speaking in Motifs

Capstone Project
2026–
Website, Installation, Take-aways

About

Breaking down Syrian and Islamic architectural motifs as a visual language, this project creates an experience that is both interactive and archival. Through a website, installation, and takeaway, it invites people to learn, engage, reinterpret, and preserve cultural forms, transforming pattern into something lived, shared, and continuously reimagined. I have learned much about time and efficiency. Took risks depending on pay out like building my first installation to increase immersive experience. I also learned how to vibe to increase efficiency and push boundaries with the website.
